# HEARTBEAT.md - Periodic Self-Check

On every heartbeat tick you review this checklist together with recent
workspace activity, then either report what needs attention or stay silent.

## Silence contract (read this first)

When nothing needs attention, your entire reply MUST be exactly:

NO_REPLY

Nothing else — no greeting, no status summary, no sign-off. Any other reply
is treated as a report and delivered to a human, so silence is the default.
When something does need attention, keep the report short and actionable:
what is wrong, why it matters, what to do next.

## What to check

The workspace owner edits this section. An empty checklist skips the tick
entirely — the agent is not woken and no tokens are spent. Keep each item
concrete enough to verify.

- <!-- Fill me in -->

## Continuity

Ticks share one continuous session: you remember your earlier ticks, so do
not re-report an issue you already reported unless something changed.
